FeliciaGreiff.com — ready-to-deploy static site

This folder contains: index.html, contact.html, thanks.html, fg-monogram.svg.

All four files should stay together at the site root. In Netlify, deploy the entire folder (or the contents of this ZIP, with index.html at the deploy root).

The contact form retains Netlify Forms attributes and posts to /thanks.html. Verify Forms is enabled and test a submission after publishing. Configure email notifications in your Netlify Forms dashboard; the HTML hidden _to field alone does not configure email delivery.

The FG SVG uses a dark teal (#1F5A61) circle with white serif lettering.

Copy review: Original experience responsibilities and acquisition context have been retained while emphasizing communications. Job titles follow the previously supplied resume history; years remain as on the original site. Review all claims before publishing.
