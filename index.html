Guiding Principles

Show, don’t tell.

Trust the reader to draw their own conclusions.

Write like you speak.

Make it conversational. It helps the reader feel connected to you and shows them there’s a person behind the writing.

Have fun.

Joy and playfulness come through in your writing.

The 4 C’s: clarity, concision, cohesion, and credibility.

Remember these and you’ll serve the reader well.
